# Is the compactness confound method-independent?

AUC = probability that the real catalytic site ranks above a decoy (0.5 = chance). Decoys replace the target's catalytic residues; 5 per pair per family. RMSD-based methods: lower RMSD ranks higher. 95% CI: pair-level bootstrap. Mapping baselines use k = min(m, n) residues; the random-mapping value is the median RMSD of 200 random injective mappings.
